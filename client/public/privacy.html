Job Tracker — Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2026

Job Tracker is a personal job-application tracking tool. This page explains what data it accesses and how that data is used and stored.

Gmail access

Job Tracker requests read-only access to Gmail (gmail.readonly) for one purpose: scanning for emails from job application systems (e.g. Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, and direct employer senders) to automatically detect application status updates — applied, interviewing, offer, or rejected.

  • Job Tracker never sends, deletes, or modifies email, and never requests write access to Gmail.
  • Only the sender, subject, and body text of messages matching known job-application senders are read; other mail is not processed.
  • Extracted information (company name, job title, status, and a short excerpt used as a note) is stored in Job Tracker's own database, tied to the account that connected Gmail.

What is not done with your data

  • Data is not sold, shared, or used for advertising.
  • Data is not used to train any AI/ML model.
  • Gmail data is not shared with any third party, except an optional, low-confidence classification fallback sent to Google's Gemini API (email subject/body text only, for classification, not stored by that service beyond the request).

Data storage and retention

Application data is stored in a private database operated by the Job Tracker account holder. Gmail access tokens are encrypted at rest and are never stored in plaintext. You can revoke Job Tracker's access at any time from your Google Account permissions page.
